# Ski Week 2027 options board

## Purpose

The first tab helps the group choose between four viable resorts before detailed flight, transfer and accommodation research begins.

## Shortlist

1. **St Anton am Arlberg** — the most memorable skiing and après, with a deliberate Lech/Zürs strategy needed for newer skiers.
2. **La Plagne / Paradiski** — the easiest group fit and strongest snow confidence, with a longer transfer and less village character.
3. **Saalbach-Hinterglemm** — the best-balanced Austrian option: approachable circuits, lively huts and straightforward access.
4. **Mayrhofen** — a lively real town with a clean Ahorn/Penken ability split and flexible apartment value.

Kitzbühel stays outside the carousel because the earlier options review found it the weakest fit against the working budget.

## What every resort view includes

- Resort tabs at the top for direct switching.
- A moving, photo-led resort story instead of a detailed embedded map.
- A simple London-to-resort journey ribbon.
- Eight decision categories: new skiers, intermediates, confident skiers, snow confidence, après, travel, budget and best base.
- A plain-English verdict first, with one useful number or fact beneath it.
- A short recommendation and a locally saved preference button.

The decision categories follow themes commonly used by specialist ski-review sites, while the wording is adapted for this mixed-ability group.

## Budget interpretation

The figures are planning ranges rather than like-for-like live quotes:

- St Anton uses the existing full-trip working estimate.
- La Plagne uses an indicative catered core package.
- Saalbach uses an indicative catered core estimate.
- Mayrhofen shows both apartment and half-board reference points because the stay model materially changes the result.

Once the group chooses a resort, the next step is dated flight, transfer and accommodation research for eight people.

## Interaction and format

- Tab, arrow, keyboard and swipe navigation.
- One resort per view so the content stays readable on mobile.
- Subtle photo movement respects reduced-motion settings.
- The saved preference persists in the browser.
- Existing St Anton tabs remain as deeper planning material for the current frontrunner, not as a confirmed final itinerary.
