The daily ritual

Mooserwirt, 15:30, every day. The Final Countdown plays, 500 people in ski boots lose their minds, and we have a table. That's the trip.

Ski patrol notice
⛔

Group does not ski the home run.

Download the Nasserein gondola instead, walk up to the après. Jason skis it and holds the table.

Why this rule exists

The Steissbachtal home run is an icy, mogulled funnel by 3pm with the whole mountain on it. It ends more holidays than the off-piste does. Same party, zero tears.

The day, three ways

Plan A · normal day

Split mornings — lessons / Jason's steeps. Reunite for mountain lunch or 3pm après. Lech–Zürs via Flexenbahn for the group.

Plan B · flat light, wind, tired legs

Stay low + tree-lined: Gampen and Nasserein blues, long lunch, early Sennhütte. Or half-day + spa.

Plan C · no-ski fallback

WellCom spa, village walk, swim, Valluga ride-up for the view (non-skiers can), cards at the chalet, big lunch out.
