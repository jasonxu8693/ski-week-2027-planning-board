Most whole apartments sold on booking.com and VRBO are non classé, which triggers the 5% proportional rate. The gap between best and worst case on the French week is €246 across the group. Ask the owner for the classification in writing. Also check whether the platform already collected it — since 2019 they legally must for non-professional lessors, so you can be charged twice at the desk.

Flight traps · verified

  • BA London City → Innsbruck stops 9 January 2027. Runs 12 Dec – 9 Jan only. Does not operate on Sat 16 Jan.
  • Luton → Innsbruck is Sunday-only. Useless for a Saturday changeover.
  • Ski carriage differs structurally. BA absorbs skis into the checked allowance; easyJet and Jet2 charge for skis on top of a hold bag with no weight pooling.
  • Ben’s Bus charges £4 per extra item per journey. Unconfirmed whether a ski bag counts.

Saturday Innsbruck arrivals that do work: BA2618 LGW 08:25→11:25 · easyJet EZY8693 LGW 07:00→09:55 · BA0686 LHR 10:00→13:05.

Two operational moves worth making

  • Book the Val Thorens transfer private and request the Moûtiers supermarket stop at booking time. Ski-Lifts permits stops on private transfers only, with advance notice. Resort shelf prices run 10–30% higher.
  • Sherpa closes 12:30–16:00 and keys hand over "at the latest 5pm". A Saturday afternoon arrival can land with no shop open — the valley stop is the reliable fix for night one.

The shortlist · 9–16 January

Every option here was opened on its live listing and checked against the real dates — availability, price, bedrooms, bathrooms and location read from Vrbo, Airbnb or Booking, not from the spreadsheet. Priced at 10.

Val Thorens is the preferred base

Highest resort in the 3 Vallées, the most snow-sure, and you are in it rather than commuting to it. Worth paying up for.

Saint-Martin-de-Belleville has been dropped. It was the cheapest thing on the board at £387 a head, but getting out of the village is two lifts — Saint Martin 1 (a 6-minute ride) then the Express to Col de Tougnète — roughly 20–25 minutes to the ridge, 35–40 to Méribel and about an hour to Val Thorens, each way. Worse, Saint Martin 1 shuts at 16:30 in January (it only runs to 17:00 from 7 February), so you leave Val Thorens by about 15:00 and cannot ski home after après. The in-resort premium is only £81–130 a head for the week. La Tania has gone with it — same ski pass, but neither Méribel nor Val Thorens.
